Initial conversation
Why are you leaving your current role?
Four years on one platform team, and the next step I want is not open there.
I joined to build the internal developer platform from nothing, and over four years it grew from a deploy script to the paved road for forty services. That work is done in the sense that matters: the team runs it well without me in the loop, and the roadmap is now about polish rather than shape.
What I want next is a problem where the shape is still open, on a team small enough that the architecture is a conversation rather than a ticket. The migration I led last year1 was the closest I got to that here, and it is the kind of work I want to do full time.

What do you want in your next role?
Ownership of a system end to end, a say in its architecture, and a team that ships.
Three things. Owning something end to end, from the first design conversation to the on-call rota. A real seat at architecture decisions, which means being in the room early rather than reviewing the outcome. And a team that ships regularly enough that decisions get tested by users instead of by debate. Remote within the EU is the constraint, everything else is negotiable.
