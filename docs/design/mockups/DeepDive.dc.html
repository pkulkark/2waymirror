Tell me about a project you are proud of.

4 evidence

Led a sharded queue migration that cut p99 latency by 40 percent with no downtime.

I led the migration of our task queue from a single Redis instance to a sharded setup with per-tenant isolation1. Peak load had grown past what one instance could absorb, and the largest tenant's bursts were showing up as latency for everyone else.

The design gave each tenant its own shard key and moved the routing into the client library2, so no service had to change how it enqueued work. Shards could be added by config, and a tenant could be moved between shards with a drain and replay rather than a cutover.

Rollout ran tenant by tenant over six weeks, behind a flag, with the old and new paths writing in parallel and a comparison job checking the two3. p99 latency fell by 40 percent at peak, and no customer saw downtime.

What I would do differently: the comparison job was written late and found two bugs in the first week that a week of earlier shadow traffic would have caught.
